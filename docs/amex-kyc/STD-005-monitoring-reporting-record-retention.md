# STD-005 — Monitoring, Reporting & Record Retention

> Illustrative stand-in for a Devin demo. Not an American Express standard.

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Owner | GFCC Financial Intelligence Unit (internal) |
| Version | 3.0 (fictional) |

## 1. Transaction monitoring

- **§1.1** Rule-based and model-based monitoring cover all card and merchant activity. Scenarios include rapid load/spend, round-amount patterns, refund abuse, transaction laundering (merchant), and spend that does not match the declared profile.
- **§1.2** Alerts are dispositioned within 30 days. Escalations become an investigation case.
- **§1.3** Complex, unusually large or unusual-pattern transactions with no apparent economic purpose are always escalated for investigation.

## 2. Group information

- **§2.1** Where a customer has relationships with more than one Amex-group entity, the investigator reviews group-wide data, subject to the data-sharing agreement.

## 3. Suspicious activity reporting

- **§3.1** US: SARs are filed with FinCEN within 30 calendar days of initial detection.
- **§3.2** Non-US: STRs are filed with the local FIU named in the country addendum, on the local timeline.
- **§3.3 FIU / law-enforcement requests for information:** responded to within **10 business days** unless the request sets a shorter deadline.
- **§3.4** Tipping off is prohibited. Customer communications about a declined or terminated relationship use approved neutral templates.

## 4. Record retention

- **§4.1** CDD/KYB records, screening results, risk ratings and investigation records are kept for **5 years after the relationship ends**, or 5 years after an application is declined.
- **§4.2** Transaction records are kept for 5 years from the transaction date.
- **§4.3** Records are held so they can be retrieved within 5 business days for a regulator request.

## 5. Cash

- **§5.1** Amex does not accept cash for card payments. Merchant cash activity is out of scope.
