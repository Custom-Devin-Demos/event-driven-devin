**Status key.** **aligned**: the internal clause already meets the requirement. **gap**: an internal clause exists but conflicts with, or falls short of, the requirement (conflicts are marked ⚠). **new**: no internal clause covers it, so a new country-specific obligation is needed.

**UI impact** refers to the cardmember questionnaire in `/amex-kyc?market=FR`. Rows marked "none (back office)" need process or KYB-onboarding changes, not changes to this portal.

## 1. Summary

- **36 requirements**: 8 aligned, 21 gaps (11 of them conflicts ⚠), 7 new.
- **Biggest conflicts with the internal policies:**
  1. POL-001 §3.1 does **not collect place of birth** and keeps **one nationality** only. AMLR Art. 22(1)(a) requires place of birth and *all* nationalities (or statelessness / refugee / subsidiary-protection status). → FR-REQ-03, FR-REQ-04.
  2. POL-002 §4.1–§4.2 only counts **direct** holdings of 25 % and uses no multiplication method. AMLR Art. 52(1) counts **direct or indirect** holdings, multiplied through every chain and added up. → FR-REQ-15.
  3. POL-002 §4.3 / §4.6 identifies **one** control person and, if no 25 % owner exists, continues with one person. AMLR Art. 51, 53 and 22(2) require *every* person who controls through other means and, as fallback, **all** senior managing officials. → FR-REQ-16.
  4. POL-002 §4.7 makes the beneficial-ownership register **optional** and has **no discrepancy reporting**. AMLR Art. 22(7) makes consulting the central register mandatory, Art. 23(4) requires proof of registration, and Art. 24 / CMF L.561-47-1 require discrepancies to be reported within 14 calendar days. → FR-REQ-18, FR-REQ-19.
  5. STD-003 §5 allows **7 years** between reviews for Low risk (84 months). AMLR Art. 26(2) caps it at **5 years** (1 year for higher risk). → FR-REQ-22.
  6. STD-005 §3.3 gives **10 business days** to answer the FIU. AMLR Art. 69(1) requires **5 working days**, and TRACFIN may shorten it. → FR-REQ-26.
  7. POL-002 §7.2 allows Low-risk merchants **90 days** of deferred UBO verification. AMLR Art. 33(1)(a) caps simplified-due-diligence postponement at **60 days**. → FR-REQ-09.
- **New French obligations not in the pack:** TRACFIN suspicious-transaction reporting through ERMES and the duty to refrain before reporting (FR-REQ-25), TRACFIN's right to oppose a transaction (FR-REQ-27), the French national asset-freeze register (FR-REQ-21), ACPR supervision and the arrêté of 6 January 2021 (FR-REQ-30), French-language customer documents (FR-REQ-31), and the ban on collecting the NIR (FR-REQ-05, FR-REQ-32).
- **UI changes in this PR:** a new title and EU/French regulatory and privacy notices; birth surname, surname of use and place of birth; a new *Nationality* step; a French address format (5-digit code postal per territory, BP / CS / TSA / CEDEX rejected); the SSN/ITIN step replaced by an *Identity Document* step for French documents plus an optional numéro fiscal; a new *Occupation* step with the employer's SIREN; and a new *PEP declaration* step. Every new field is validated server-side (`validateStep` / `submit`) and covered in `tests/526efb4c-kyc.test.js`.
