# STD-004 — Sanctions, PEP & Adverse Media Screening

> Illustrative stand-in for a Devin demo. Not an American Express standard.

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Owner | GFCC Sanctions Compliance |
| Version | 5.1 (fictional) |

## 1. Who is screened

- **§1.1** Applicants, card members, supplementary card members, entities, beneficial owners, directors and control persons.

## 2. Lists

- **§2.1 Sanctions:** OFAC SDN and consolidated lists, UN Security Council consolidated list, EU Consolidated Financial Sanctions List, UK HMT (OFSI) list, plus any local lists named in the country addendum.
- **§2.2 PEP / RCA:** vendor PEP database, covering domestic, foreign and international-organisation PEPs, their family members and known close associates.
- **§2.3 Adverse media:** vendor negative-news screening for High-risk and EDD cases.

## 3. Frequency

- **§3.1** Screening happens at onboarding before account activation, every day against list deltas, and whenever customer data changes.

## 4. PEP handling

- **§4.1** A confirmed PEP, family member or close associate is rated High (STD-003 §3.1).
- **§4.2** Senior-management approval (GFCC Level 2 or above) is needed to establish or continue the relationship.
- **§4.3** Source of funds is documented, and source of wealth too where spend is above USD 250k per year (POL-001 §6.2).
- **§4.4** Enhanced ongoing monitoring applies: a 12-month review cycle and lower monitoring thresholds.
- **§4.5 Former PEPs:** a person who has left a prominent public function keeps PEP treatment for **12 months**. After that, the analyst may remove the PEP flag based on risk.

## 5. Sanctions hits

- **§5.1** Possible matches are worked within 1 business day. Confirmed matches are blocked or rejected and reported as required by the relevant sanctions authority.
- **§5.2** Asset freezes and targeted financial sanctions under UN/EU regimes are applied without delay once confirmed.
