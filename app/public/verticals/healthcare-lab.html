Running a demo

about 10 minutes
ResetReload the page. Every field is pre-filled with the case that fails; nothing to type.
TriggerClick the main button once. The app shows a customer-facing error and a reference number.
WatchSwitch to Slack #automated-alerts: the alert card arrives with a Devin session already investigating. The fix PR follows a few minutes later.

All brands, patients and products are fictional and all data is synthetic — no PHI. Talk tracks for each demo are in docs/healthcare/README.md.
