Frequently
asked questions

What is Nourish?

Nourish is a telehealth nutrition counseling platform that connects patients with Registered Dietitian Nutritionists (RDNs), available in all 50 states.

What is a registered dietitian? Why should I see a dietitian?

Registered Dietitian Nutritionists are health professionals specializing in food and nutrition who help you reach your long-term health goals.

Are dietitians the same thing as nutritionists?

All dietitians are nutritionists, but not all nutritionists are dietitians — RDN is a nationally recognized credential.

How many appointments will I need?

It depends on your goals; most patients meet with their dietitian regularly over several months.
