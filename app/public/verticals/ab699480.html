FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does inDrive provide the loan directly?
Yes, inDrive acts as a lender directly providing the loan through its subsidiary APT Intersolutions S.A. de C.V.
Where can I check my application status?
After tapping ‘Get a loan’ in the app, you’ll see your tailored offer. Just confirm and complete registration
Where to check the loan terms?
Click 'Get a loan' to view key terms, including interest rates and the amount, clearly displayed.
What is the CAT for the cash loan?
The final CAT will be determined during the application process and shown in the loan contract
Is there a monthly minimum payment?
Yes, your monthly payment will be clearly stated in your offer, based on the loan term
How to repay the loan?
You pay a fixed percentage of earnings for each completed ride, or pay directly by bank transfer via SPEI when ride payouts don’t cover the monthly minimum payment.

APT Intersolutions, S.A. de C.V. is an entity supervised by the Federal Consumer Protection Agency (PROFECO) and obliged to comply with the provisions of the Law for Transparency and Regulation of Financial Services.
