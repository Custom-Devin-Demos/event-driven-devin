    Savings card questions

    Who can use a manufacturer savings card?

    Patients with commercial insurance. Cards can't be used with Medicare, Medicaid or other government plans.

    Can I use a savings card on a 90-day supply?

    Yes, where the manufacturer's program covers it. The card is applied after your insurance pays its share.

    What if my card is rejected?

    Your pharmacist can retry the claim or contact the program's help desk. You won't be charged the card amount until it's resolved.
