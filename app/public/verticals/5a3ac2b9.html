Labs

Made in the room, not in the cloud.

A24 Labs builds small, opinionated tools for our filmmakers. Everything here was shaped on a real production, runs on footage we own, and never trains on anyone else's. It exists to get you to the set faster with a clearer picture in your head.

Try Previs
Previs

Paste a scene. Get a board.

Previs reads a scene from your draft, breaks it into coverage, and returns a numbered shot board with lens, framing, and a still for each setup — in your frame format, in your look. Directors use it the night before. DPs use it to argue with directors the night before.

Script Reader 2026

Coverage notes on a draft in the voice of our story team: what's alive, what's stalling, what the third act owes the first.

Dailies Notes 2026

Overnight, every take gets a line: circled, performance, continuity flags. The editor wakes up to a rough assembly plan.

Sound Bed 2026

Temp score and room tone that sit under a scene without arguing with it — cleared, ours, and gone when the composer shows up.
