Hospital bill benefits

Enjoy the potential benefit of a prompt payment discount by paying your hospital bill in full. For more information, please reach out to our customer service team.

All transactions via Children’s Minnesota online are eligible for a refund if an overpayment is made. Please allow 10-14 business days for a refund.
