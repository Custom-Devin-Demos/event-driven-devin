Questions about homeowner loans.

Will checking my eligibility affect my credit score?

No. We use a soft search, which you can see on your credit file but other lenders can't.

Can I apply if I'm self-employed?

Yes. We regularly lend to sole traders, company directors and contractors. Use your latest net profit as your annual income.

What can I use a homeowner loan for?

Home improvements, consolidating debts, or a large purchase — we'll talk it through with you.
