Challenges

Prior authorization shouldn't be where patient care gets stuck.

The Hardest Questions Still Require Manual Chart Review

EHR-generated requests may prefill basic patient, prescriber, and drug information, but the clinical questions that determine approval still send staff back into the chart. Teams search through notes, labs, diagnoses, and treatment history one question at a time.

Every Payer Plays by Different Rules

Forms, clinical criteria, submission channels, and medication-specific requirements vary by payer.

Submission Isn't the Finish Line

After an authorization goes out, someone still has to track the response, follow up when it stalls, understand a denial, gather additional documentation, and start an appeal.

Why Plenful

We Don't Just Submit Authorizations. We Manage the Work.

Plenful's Prior Authorization solution manages prior authorization from preparation through approval or appeal. It finds the right requirements, pulls supporting evidence from the patient record, prepares the submission with cited sources, sends it through the appropriate channel, and keeps the next action moving until the case is resolved.

Spend Less Time Digging the Chart

Plenful reads across the patient record to find the clinical evidence required for payer-specific questions and pre-fills the authorization with cited sources. Your team can verify the answer instead of searching the chart from scratch.

One Workflow Across Medical and Pharmacy Benefit

Manage authorizations across both benefit pathways without creating separate processes for each one. Plenful brings the work, status, and next steps together so your team knows where every case stands.

Keep Every Authorization Moving Through Resolution

Plenful tracks what happens after submission, prompts follow-up when a case needs attention, and routes denials into an appeals workflow with the reason and supporting documentation attached. The work doesn't disappear into a payer queue after you hit submit.
