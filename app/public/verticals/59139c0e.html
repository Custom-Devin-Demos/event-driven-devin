Benefits of Automated Tax Withholding

Accurate Payments On Time, Every Time

Imagine no longer scrambling to manage payment schedules or worrying about late withholding payments. Sovos Withholding Compliance tracks payment frequencies and automates the payment delivery to the IRS, states and localities. Stay compliant, avoid costly penalties, and free up your team to focus on what truly matters: driving your business forward.

Liability Import

Import tax liabilities from any payroll or source system for streamlined processing.

Payment Schedules

Manage standard or customized payment frequencies and thresholds for any jurisdiction.

Credit and Reversal Tracking

Track credits and reversals to maintain accurate and compliant payment records.

Simplify your workload

Manually managing tax withholding payments and filing forms like 940, 941, 945, and 1042 is time-consuming and prone to errors, leading to costly penalties. Sovos Withholding Compliance automates the remittance and filing process, reducing errors, saving up to 75% of manual effort, and ensuring accuracy. Let your team focus on higher-value work while confidently staying compliant with tax obligations.

Federal Payments

Electronically deliver federal payments via EFTPS.

State and Local Payment

Deliver state and local ACH payments via NACHA payment network, where accepted.

Filing Returns

Electronically file 940, 941, 945, 1042 and state and local equivalent forms.

Stay compliant, and stay confident

Sovos Withholding Compliance ensures all tax withholding data is fully reconciled—from source systems to payments, filing returns, and information returns. It quickly identifies out-of-balance information return records, minimizing the chance for regulators to question payments or return processes. With comprehensive activity tracking and regulator confirmations for audit defense, Sovos provides unmatched accuracy, protection, and confidence in compliance.

Source System Reconciliation

Ensures withheld taxes reported in source systems match amounts remitted and reported.

Form-Level Matching

Matches payments and return filing amounts with W-2/1099/1042-S reported withholding amounts to identify discrepancies.

Audit Trails

Tracks all user activities, and payment and return filing confirmations for transparency and audit defense.
