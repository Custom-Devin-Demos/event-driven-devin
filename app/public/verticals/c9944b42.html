Report an Outage

To report an outage, call 1-800-PGE-5002 (1-800-743-5002) or enter your address below. If you smell natural gas, see downed powerlines or suspect another emergency situation, leave the area immediately and call 9-1-1. Then, call PG&E at 1-800-743-5000.
