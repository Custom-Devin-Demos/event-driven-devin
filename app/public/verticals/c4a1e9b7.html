What does comprehensive car insurance cover?

Comprehensive cover is our highest level of car insurance. As well as covering damage to other people's cars and property, it covers your own car against accidental damage, fire and theft.

Theft, fire and accidental damage

Repairs or replacement for your own car, even if the accident was your fault.

Other people's cars and property

Cover for damage you cause to other vehicles, property and injury to other people.

Lifetime guarantee on repairs

On repairs made by our approved repairers, for as long as you own the car.

Uninsured drivers promise

Keep your no claims discount if you're hit by an uninsured driver.

European cover

Same level of cover for up to 90 days a year driving in the EU.

Windscreen, key and audio cover

Replacement keys, sat-nav and audio, and windscreen repairs.
