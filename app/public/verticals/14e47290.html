Crumbs, that’s good.

You decide how long you want to spread the cost of your phone, and you can chop and change your monthly data as often as you like. It’s totally up to you.
With Anytime Upgrade Flex you’re in control, with a contract that rolls however you roll.

Here’s how it works

We split your plan into two contracts:

  • 1 for your phone
  • 1 for your monthly usage

You pay for both together, in one monthly direct debit.

1. Choose how to spread the cost of your phone

  • Either 12, 18, 24 or 36 months.
  • You pay the same for your phone whatever length you choose.

Once your phone’s paid off you can either drop down to just your monthly data plan payments, or decide to upgrade. It’s up to you.

2. Pick a monthly data plan


Decide how much data you’d like.

(You get unlimited calls and texts).

It’s a 1 month rolling contract.

3. Flex it whenever you want

You can have more or less data each month if you want.

And if you want to pay off your phone early and upgrade, you can do that too.

Flex your plan

Move your monthly data up or down. Changes take effect from your next bill date.

Putting you in control and saving you dough. At Tesco Mobile, that’s how we roll.

If you cancel your usage contract before the end of your phone contract, you will need to pay off the remaining balance of your phone credit agreement. Because you’re on a 1-month rolling usage contract, you’ll only have to pay for the usage for the month you’re in.
