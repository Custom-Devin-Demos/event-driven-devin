Before you begin

5 minutes estimated time to complete.

Introduction

This digital service allows you to renew the road tax for your vehicle for either 6 or 12 months as part of your vehicle ownership tax.

What you need

Ensure that your vehicle meets all the renewal prerequisites at least 1 working day before renewing your road tax.

Accepted payment methods

eNETSVISAMastercardPayNowApple PayGoogle Pay
  • Please allow pop-ups for eNETS and your banks' websites to avoid "time-out" errors.
  • Ensure that you have adequate balance and daily payment limit for the amount to be debited from your account.
