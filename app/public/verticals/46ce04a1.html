How the Veloxa Savings Card works

One card, every fill. Patients show it once; pharmacies keep it on file as secondary coverage.

For patients

Activate once online or by phone. Your card is ready to use the same day.

  1. Activate your card

    Answer three eligibility questions and get your Cardholder ID right away, or call 1-833-555-0199.

  2. Show it at the pharmacy

    Give your pharmacist the card with your prescription and insurance card. Mail-order pharmacies accept it too.

  3. Pay as little as $25

    The card covers your out-of-pocket cost above $25, up to $7,200 a year, on 30-day and 90-day fills.

For pharmacists

Process the Veloxa Savings Card as a secondary claim after the primary commercial claim adjudicates.

Questions about the savings card

Using your card

Who can use the Veloxa Savings Card?

U.S. residents 18 or older with commercial insurance that covers Veloxa and a valid prescription for type 2 diabetes. The card can't be used by patients whose prescriptions are paid in whole or in part by Medicare, Medicaid, TRICARE, VA or any other federal or state program, or by cash-paying patients.

How much can I save?

You may pay as little as $25 per 30-day or 90-day fill. The card pays the difference between your out-of-pocket cost and $25, up to $7,200 per calendar year. Any amount above the yearly maximum is your responsibility.

Can I use it for a 90-day supply?

Yes. Starting with the 2026 program year, the Veloxa 90-day pack (3 pens) is covered at retail and mail-order pharmacies. Your pharmacist processes it the same way as a 30-day fill.

Does the card expire?

The card is valid for 12 fills per calendar year and renews automatically each January while the program is active and you remain eligible. Veloria may change or end the program at any time without notice.

Terms and conditions

Program terms
  • Offer valid only for commercially insured patients with a Veloxa prescription for an FDA-approved indication.
  • Patients may not seek reimbursement from any health savings, flexible spending or other healthcare reimbursement account for the amount covered by the card.
  • Card is not insurance and is not transferable. It may not be combined with any other coupon, discount or free-trial offer.
  • Void where prohibited by law, taxed or restricted. Veloria reserves the right to rescind, revoke or amend the program at any time.
What if the card is rejected at the counter?

Ask your pharmacist to confirm the RxBIN, RxPCN and RxGRP on the card and to submit the claim as secondary coverage. If it is still rejected, the pharmacist can call the Veloxa Pharmacy Help Desk at 1-833-555-0199 with the reject code and reference number shown on their screen.

Can I use the card with a mail-order pharmacy?

Yes. Give the mail-order pharmacy the card details when you set up your prescription. Most mail-order pharmacies apply it automatically to every refill.

How is my information used?

Veloria uses the information you provide to run the savings program, confirm eligibility and process claims. See the Veloria Privacy Notice for details. This demonstration site uses synthetic data only.

Indication and usage

Veloxa (veloratide) injection 1.5 mg is a fictional once-weekly prescription medicine used along with diet and exercise to improve blood sugar (glucose) in adults with type 2 diabetes. It is not known if Veloxa is safe and effective in children. Veloxa is not for use in people with type 1 diabetes. This page is a demonstration; it does not describe a real medicine.

Do not use Veloxa if

  • you or any of your family have ever had a type of thyroid cancer called medullary thyroid carcinoma, or you have Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2.
  • you are allergic to veloratide or any of the ingredients in Veloxa.

Important Safety Information

Warning: possible thyroid tumors, including cancer

Tell your healthcare provider if you get a lump or swelling in your neck, hoarseness, trouble swallowing or shortness of breath. In studies with rodents, medicines of this class caused thyroid tumors. It is not known if Veloxa causes thyroid tumors in people.

Veloxa may cause serious side effects, including

  • Inflammation of the pancreas. Stop using Veloxa and call your healthcare provider right away if you have severe pain in your stomach area that will not go away, with or without vomiting.
  • Low blood sugar. Your risk may be higher if you also take insulin or a sulfonylurea. Signs include dizziness, shakiness, sweating, confusion and fast heartbeat.
  • Kidney problems. Diarrhea, nausea and vomiting may cause loss of fluids, which can make kidney problems worse.
  • Serious allergic reactions and gallbladder problems, including gallstones.

Most common side effects

Nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, decreased appetite, constipation, indigestion and stomach pain. Tell your healthcare provider about all the medicines you take and if you are pregnant, plan to become pregnant or are breastfeeding.

You are encouraged to report negative side effects of prescription drugs to the FDA. Visit www.fda.gov/medwatch or call 1-800-FDA-1088.
