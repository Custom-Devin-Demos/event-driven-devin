Capital activity

When a fund issues a capital call, limited partners move committed capital from a funding account to the fund’s capital account by the stated due date. Submissions made here are applied to the selected account and confirmed on this page once the transfer is accepted.

Choose the source and destination accounts, enter the amount called, and select a settlement option. Standard settlement applies to most capital calls; priority settlement is available for same-day deadlines.

Move committed capital to a fund account. Amounts are in U.S. dollars.
