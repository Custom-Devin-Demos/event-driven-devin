Tips for a strong Passcode

  • Use a phrase only you would know, mixing upper- and lowercase letters, numbers and symbols.
  • Don't use your name, birthday, Online ID or account numbers.
  • Don't reuse a Passcode from another website.
  • Your new Passcode also works in the Mobile Banking app. Saved Face ID or fingerprint sign-in will ask for it once.
