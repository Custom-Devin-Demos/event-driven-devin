Alerts are sent as a courtesy and don't replace your statements. Alerts may be delayed or not delivered due to circumstances beyond our control, such as carrier or network outages. Balance alerts reflect your available balance at the time the alert is generated, which may not include pending transactions. Message and data rates may apply to text alerts. Push notifications require the Bank of America Mobile Banking app with notifications turned on. Text STOP to 73981 to cancel text alerts or HELP for help.
