How BankAmeriDeals works

  1. Activate deals you like in Online or Mobile Banking.
  2. Pay with your eligible Bank of America debit or credit card before the deal expires.
  3. Earn cash back, credited to your account by the end of the following month.

BankAmeriDeals offers are available to Bank of America consumer debit and credit cardholders with an active Online Banking profile. Cash back is based on the purchase amount, excluding taxes and tips where identifiable, and is limited to the maximum shown. Purchases must be made in a single transaction and may not qualify if made through a third-party payment service, mobile wallet or as a split payment. Offers can't be combined with other BankAmeriDeals for the same merchant. Cash back earned is reported on Form 1099-MISC if it totals $600 or more in a calendar year. Bank of America isn't responsible for the products or services of participating merchants.
