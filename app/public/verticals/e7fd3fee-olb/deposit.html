Tips for a successful deposit

  • Place the check on a dark, well-lit surface.
  • Make sure all four corners are visible.
  • Endorse the back: sign and write “For Mobile Deposit Only at Bank of America”.
  • Keep the check for 14 days, then destroy it securely.

Funds availability

Deposits made before 9 p.m. ET on a business day are received that day. Up to $225.00 is available immediately; the rest is generally available the next business day, or the second business day for larger checks.
