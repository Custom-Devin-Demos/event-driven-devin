Security tips

  • We'll never call to ask for your one-time code. If someone asks, hang up and call the number on the back of your card.
  • Watch for Zelle® scams. Only send money to people you know and trust. Bank of America will never ask you to send money to yourself.
  • Use a unique Passcode you don't use for other sites, and change it if you think it's been shared.
  • Check your SiteKey every time you log in. If it's missing or wrong, don't enter your Passcode.
  • Turn on alerts for large purchases and new-device sign-ins so you know right away if something's wrong.
