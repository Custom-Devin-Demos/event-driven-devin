Payroll cycle

From approval to salary disbursement

Every cycle follows the governance framework agreed with your payroll service team, with responsibilities and approvals defined up front.

  • 1
    Master data

    Employee records, allowances and deductions are confirmed against the cycle calendar.

  • 2
    Calculation and review

    Payroll is calculated and reviewed with your finance team before approval.

  • 3
    Disbursement

    Salaries are released to employee accounts from the funding account you select.

  • 4
    Compliance reporting

    Wage Protection System files and cycle reports are filed after settlement.
