More flexibility with payouts on demand

Choose when to get paid

Sellers paid on a weekly, every-two-weeks or monthly schedule don’t have to wait for the next scheduled date. Move your available funds on demand whenever you need them, and they reach your bank account on its usual processing timeline, generally 1-3 business days.

Get funds in a flash

When timing matters, send available funds to a linked Visa or Mastercard debit card with an express payout. Most express payouts land in about 30 minutes.1

Enjoy simple, easy payouts

A payout request takes only a few clicks, so your money moves on your timeline.

Here’s how it works:

  • In Seller Hub, open the Payments tab and select Request Payout beside Available funds.
  • Enter the amount you want to send.
  • Pick your bank account (generally 1-3 business days at no extra cost) or your debit card (about 30 minutes1, fee applies).
  • Confirm with Send my money now.

Learn more about payouts on demand.
Please note: Sellers on a daily payout schedule already receive the fastest scheduled option, so on-demand requests aren’t offered on that schedule.

1 A fee is charged on each express payout to a debit card. Eligibility varies by seller.
