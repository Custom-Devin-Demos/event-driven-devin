Upgrading your phone or tablet

Ready for a new device? If you’re on O2 Refresh, you can upgrade early. Just pay off your Device Plan as soon as you like and choose the new phone or tablet you want. And with our custom plans, you can design your new plan to suit your needs.

FAQs

I’m a Carphone Warehouse customer, how do I upgrade?

If you took out your O2 contract through Carphone Warehouse, you can upgrade with us directly. Sign in to My O2 to see your options or visit an O2 store.

When can I upgrade?

On O2 Refresh you can upgrade at any time once your Device Plan is paid off. On a custom plan you can upgrade early for a fee, or at the end of your Device Plan.

When will my new device be delivered?

Standard delivery is free and usually takes 2–3 working days. Premium delivery is £9.99.

What are custom plans?

Custom plans split your bill into a Device Plan and an Airtime Plan, so you can choose how long you pay for your device and change your Airtime Plan separately.

How do I sign in to My O2?

Use your O2 username and password on the web or in the My O2 app. If you’ve forgotten your details, use the reset link on the sign-in page.

How do I get in touch if I need help?

Visit our Help pages, use the My O2 app, or call 202 free from your O2 mobile.
