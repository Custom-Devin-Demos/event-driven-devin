Blue Rewards

Get access to enhanced rates on savings, cashback on everyday spending, and an Apple TV subscription – all for just £5 a month. Cancel any time.

What you get

Boost your Barclays Bank Account with Blue Rewards. Eligibility, terms and conditions apply.

  • Apple TV

    Unlimited access to Apple TV worth £9.99 a month. Activate it in the Barclays app.

  • Barclays Cashback Rewards

    Earn cashback at selected retailers with a linked Barclays Visa debit or credit card.

  • 3.96% AER

    Rainy Day Saver

    Our highest instant-access rate of 3.96% AER / 3.89% gross p.a. on balances from £1 to £5,000.

  • 2.51% AER

    Blue Rewards Saver

    2.51% AER / 2.48% gross p.a. (variable) in months where you don’t dip into your savings.

Join Blue Rewards

Add Blue Rewards to your Barclays Bank Account. The £5 monthly fee is taken from your nominated account.

1. Your account

Only one person on a joint account can join.

2. Your rewards

Activation takes a few minutes and needs an Apple Account.

3. Check you’re eligible

  • At least £800 paid into your nominated account every month
  • Registered for the Barclays app, with an email address
  • Aged 18 or over and a UK resident
