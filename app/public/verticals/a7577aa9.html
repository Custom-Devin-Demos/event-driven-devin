FAQ

What is AI governance and why does it matter for enterprise organizations?

AI governance is the set of policies, processes, and controls an organization uses to make sure AI systems are developed and used responsibly, securely, and in line with regulation.

What is AI governance software?

AI governance software helps teams inventory AI, assess risk, apply policy, and produce evidence that controls are working across the AI lifecycle.

What should an enterprise AI governance framework include?

An AI inventory, consistent risk assessment, clear ownership, policy-driven controls, runtime monitoring, and audit-ready documentation.

How can we comply with the EU AI Act and other global AI regulations?

Map each AI use case to its regulatory obligations, apply framework templates during assessment, and keep evidence current as systems change.

How does OneTrust govern AI at runtime?

OneTrust connects runtime signals from supported AI platforms to policy, detects violations, and enforces guardrails where AI runs.

What is the difference between AI Policy Manager and Runtime Controls?

AI Policy Manager defines policy intent, scope, and required controls. Runtime Controls execute the technical actions that enforce that policy in production.
