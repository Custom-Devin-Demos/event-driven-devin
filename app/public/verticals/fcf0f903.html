How the Aravia Copay Savings Program works

One enrollment covers benefits verification, copay assistance and coordination with your specialty pharmacy — so therapy starts on the date your prescriber intended.

1

Verify benefits

Your access coordinator confirms coverage with your plan and PBM and places your record in the program tier it qualifies for.

2

Enroll in assistance

Commercially insured patients enroll in the Copay Savings Program; uninsured and underinsured patients are referred to the Aravia Cares Foundation.

3

Fill with confidence

Your specialty pharmacy applies the benefit at the counter. Annual caps and reverification dates are tracked for you.

Specialty therapies, supported end to end

Veltrixa®, Nuvexa® and Corvalis® are dispensed through a limited network of specialty pharmacies. The Patient Access Hub coordinates prior authorization, appeals and shipment so nothing falls between your prescriber, your plan and your pharmacy.

  • Prior authorization and appeals handled by your coordinator
  • Bridge supply while coverage decisions are pending
  • Nurse support line for administration and side-effect questions

Science first. Access always.

Aravia Therapeutics develops targeted therapies in oncology, immunology and rare disease. Every launch ships with a patient-access program on day one, because a therapy a patient cannot afford is a therapy that does not work.

The Aravia Cares Foundation has provided more than 40,000 patient-years of therapy at no cost since 2018.
