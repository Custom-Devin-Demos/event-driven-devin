Client allocations & settlement

Executed blocks are allocated from the desk’s average-price account to each client’s custodian account and released for settlement. Post-trade confirms are issued when the allocation books.

Standard-lane allocations settle delivery-versus-payment on the regular cycle. Pre-cleared lanes are reserved for clients with a standing prime-broker arrangement.
