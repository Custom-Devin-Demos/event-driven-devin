Top questions

What is the Send Abroad service?

Send Abroad lets you transfer money from the UAE to other countries using the payment methods added to your Careem app. It's a quick and easy way to support family, friends, or commitments back home.

Is Careem licensed for international transfers?

Careem Pay provides international money transfers in partnership with a licensed exchange house. The service is authorized and regulated by the Central Bank of the UAE.

How long does it take for the recipient to receive the money?

Most transfers are verified within 15 minutes, and the recipient usually receives the money instantly after that. We operate 24/7, including weekends and holidays.

Are there any limits on sending money abroad?

Yes. From your Careem Wallet: minimum AED 5 per transfer, maximum AED 25,000 per day and up to 3 transfers per day. Card limits vary by destination country.

Why is my transaction still in progress?

Identity or receiver verification may still be in progress, or the receiving bank may be delayed in accepting funds. We will notify you as soon as the transfer is complete.
