Which companies have both a divestiture in play and a Buy-Side / Consolidator profile?
See my work

Only one company in the matrix screens as both an active divestor and a consolidator: Halcyon Remit.

  • Divestiture in play: exiting its legacy agent-location network; a definitive sale agreement was referenced on the Q2 call, pending regulatory approvals.
  • Consolidator profile: the pending ~$500M BorderLink acquisition, currently delayed by a suspended state money-transmitter approval, plus a stated M&A agenda in the S-4.

Three other names show portfolio-action signals but screen as sell-side or special-committee situations rather than acquirers: Quarry Commerce, Brightwater Card and Wren Payments.
