A fully managed, end-to-end payments solution. Ripple handles the infrastructure so you can launch and scale cross-border payment services without building from scratch, settling in fiat or stablecoins.

*Availability of products can vary and may be provided by different Ripple entities

Support for Multiple Stablecoins

Pay in, pay out, and settle in RLUSD and other stablecoins, or fiat — whichever asset your business requires. The settlement layer is decoupled from any single issuer’s token, with new stablecoins added as market demand develops. No single-issuer dependency, no infrastructure rebuild necessary.

First- and Third-Party Payouts Across 60+ Markets

Access last-mile rails across 60+ markets, the same network that has processed over $100 billion in payments volume. Execute real-time first- and third-party payouts in fiat or stablecoin through a single integration, without stitching together multiple vendors or relying on external payout networks.

Licensed Infrastructure Built In

The Ripple Group holds 85+ global licenses, including a NYDFS Trust Company Charter, the regulatory foundation under which RLUSD is issued, and payments licenses that enable Ripple to move money on behalf of customers across regulated financial rails globally. Our customers have a single point of contact to manage their payment needs.

Managed Stablecoin Acceptance

Accept stablecoins and sweep funds into a single consolidated settlement account, automatically. No specialist infrastructure or new bank accounts required.
