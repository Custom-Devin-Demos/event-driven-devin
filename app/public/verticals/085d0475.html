    About these scores

    • PAYDEXDollar-weighted payment performance from trade experiences, from 1 to 100. 80 means bills are paid on terms.
    • Delinquency PredictorLikelihood the business pays in a severely delinquent manner over the next 12 months.
    • Failure ScoreLikelihood the business ceases operations or seeks legal relief from creditors over the next 12 months.
    • Recommended limitA conservative and an aggressive limit based on peer and trade data. Decisions above it are referred for review.
