Lowest Price Guarantee

We always want our fans to choose Gametime as their first ticket choice. If you buy with us and find a lower price elsewhere, we will hook you up with 110% of the difference!

For example, if you paid $150 at Gametime and a competitor has the same available seats for $100, you’ll get $55 in Gametime credit to use towards your next purchase. Double the fun!

To submit a Price Match Guarantee claim, you’ll need to contact us within two hours of your purchase. Don’t wait before it’s too late! Use the form below to file a claim. The credit issued can be up to $200.

Event Cancellation Protection

Cancelled events are a huge bummer. But Gametime’s got your back! If your event was canceled and not rescheduled, you’ll automatically receive a full refund back to your original method of payment. You won't have to wait long to see your funds back – just 5 to 7 business days, on average! Plus, if you used Gametime credit, it's back in your account immediately, ready for your next adventure. You paid what you did, and that's what you'll get back – because at Gametime, we play fair.

Gametime works hard to verify cancellations as quickly as possible to keep you in the loop and your plans flexible.

If you suspect your event may be canceled or rescheduled, feel free to contact us to take a look! Read more about how Gametime helps with Changes to Your Event.

Job Loss Assurance

Should the unexpected happen and you find yourself without a job, Gametime has your back. Simply chat with us or call us at 888-355-0132, and we'll sort you out with a return for your original purchase value. Just remember, we can only accept valid state or federal unemployment forms as proof of job loss.

If a claim is validated, you will receive a refund for the full amount of the purchase. A job loss claim must be made at least 72 hours prior to the event start date. It is at Gametime’s sole discretion if a claim is determined to be valid.

On-Time Ticket Delivery

You've got our word: your tickets are our top priority. We guarantee they'll be in your hands in time for your event, and they’ll be valid for entry. If you ever have any issues, reach out to us immediately! Our team is happy to help, ensuring we will find a resolution to make it right!
