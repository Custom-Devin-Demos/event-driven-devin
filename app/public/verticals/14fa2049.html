About this voucher

eCapitaVoucher is the digital shopping voucher for CapitaLand malls in Singapore. Buy it in the CapitaStar eStore, hold it in your CapitaStar wallet, and scan it at the till at participating stores. Balances can be split across several visits until the voucher expires.
