Need more details about your claim?

Helpful information on what you’ll need for each type of claim.

Life Insurance

You’ll need the policy number if you have it, a copy of the death certificate and details of the person making the claim.

Over 50s Insurance

We’ll ask for the policyholder’s details, the death certificate and the details of the person dealing with their estate.

Terminal Illness Claim

We’ll ask for details of the diagnosis and your treating specialist so we can request medical evidence.

Critical Illness Claim

Tell us the condition, the date of diagnosis and the hospital or specialist involved.

Income Protection Claim

We’ll need details of your employer, when you stopped working and your GP or specialist.
