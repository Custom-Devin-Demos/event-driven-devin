How does billing work?

Subscription based billing

Opportunity Alerts subscriptions are billed monthly. Your billing date is based on the day you signed up. For example, if you subscribe on September 2nd, you’ll be billed on the 2nd of each month. If you subscribe on a date that doesn’t occur every month, like January 31st, we’ll bill you on the last day of the month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Nextdoor Opportunity Alerts?

Nextdoor Opportunity Alerts helps you connect with potential new customers on Nextdoor—right when they need you. When neighbors post asking for recommendations for a pro like you, you’ll get notified in real-time so you can respond quickly and be considered for new jobs and projects.

How do I purchase Opportunity Alerts?

Sign in to your business account and go to your dashboard. In the ‘Opportunities’ section, click ‘Access all’ to subscribe. You can cancel your subscription anytime from your settings.

Opportunity Alerts are currently available to businesses in select service categories. To subscribe, your business must offer services like lawn care, house cleaning, painting, plumbing, pest control, and more.

Can I choose the service area where I will receive Opportunity Alerts?

Yes. When you sign up, you’ll choose the street address that best represents the center of your service area. This doesn’t need to be your business’s physical address - it can be any address that helps us match you with the right Opportunities. We’ll send you Opportunity Alerts from neighbors posting within a 20-mile radius of that location.

How do I ensure that I'll receive the best-fit Opportunities for my business?

We’ll match you with posts based on the service categories and service area you’ve selected—the more accurate your settings, the more relevant the Opportunities will be.

What should I include in my outreach to neighbors to help me land jobs and projects?

A thoughtful reply can help your business stand out and build trust with neighbors. Introduce yourself, mention the type of work they said they need help with, share your experience, and add contact info so the neighbor can follow up.
