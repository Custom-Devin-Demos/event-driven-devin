Transfer miles to another member

Effortlessly transfer extra miles you're not sure what to do with to another MileagePlus member. Mile transfers cost $7.50 USD per 500 miles, plus a processing fee of $30.00 per transaction.

Who are you sending miles to?
The recipient's name must match the name on their MileagePlus account.
How many miles?
In increments of 500, from 500 to 100,000 miles.

Miles transfers are final and cannot be reversed. Transferred miles are subject to the MileagePlus program rules.
