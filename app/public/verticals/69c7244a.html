Frequently Asked Questions

Who can use the Consultant Portal?

The Consultant Portal is currently only available to US-based W2 Consultants. If that is not you, please continue to use our external Job Board here.

How do I register or log in to the Consultant Portal?

Go to https://my.insightglobal.com/

Enter the personal email address that you used for your Insight Global onboarding, then select “No account? Create one”.

You will receive a confirmation code in your email that you can use to complete your registration.

When is the annual open enrollment period for benefits?

Open enrollment for benefits is usually in late October/early November. If you’re a new employee or an employee with a qualifying life event (adoption, birth, marriage, etc.), please visit UKG to enroll or make changes.

Who do I reach out to if I do not know or can’t get in touch with my IG Point of Contact?

Email consultantexperience@insightglobal.com

How do I enter my time?

Our main timecard entry portal is eRecruit. However, you may be supporting a client that requires entry into a secondary proprietary system.

You should also have an email in your inbox from IG Consultant Experience containing a “How to Enter Time” one pager.

When will I receive my paycheck?

Typically, you would receive a direct deposit on Friday of the week after you submitted the hours. For example:

1. Start on Monday.
2. Submit your hours on Friday afternoon.
3. Your first payment should be sent out as a direct deposit on Friday of your second week.
4. If you have not received any payment by this day (via live check or direct deposit), please contact your IG Recruiter.

** Please note: this is a tentative calendar; dates indicated are subject to change during a holiday week. Additionally, if bank information is entered incorrectly, a direct deposit will not be sent out immediately.

Where can I view my paystubs? Where can I change my address? Where can I find an electronic W2?

UKG which you can access at https://e43.ultipro.com/.

You will not lose access to UKG after termination, and you can update your address by logging into your UltiPro account, the same way you view your pay statements. If you need assistance with this login information, please email: IGCares@insightglobal.com

Who do I reach out to if I am locked out of my UKG account?

Please contact: IGCares@insightglobal.com

Am I eligible for a 401k?

Yes, at day 89 of assignment.

You will not be automatically enrolled. You must register and enroll through Fidelity NetBenefits.

You can adjust your deductions at any time, and it will take 2 pay cycles for the changes to take effect.

Who do I reach out to if I have a question about Insight Global benefits?

Please contact: benefits@insightglobal.com

All employees are eligible for benefits starting at day 31 of assignment based on your start date

What do I do if I need my employment with Insight Global verified?

Please visit Insight Global’s Employment Verification page for details on how to verify employment.

What is Insight Global's Employer Identification Number (EIN)?

Insight Global’s Employer Identification Number is: 208775560

If my assignment ends, will Insight Global help me find another job?

Yes, we are dedicated to supporting you in this area. Please also navigate to the Job Board where you can search thousands of available job listings.

Who do I reach out to if I have a question about taxes?

Please contact: payrolltax@insightglobal.com

Step 1

Search

Connect

We’ll chat about your experience, your goals, and what your ideal job looks like. Then we’ll help prep you for interviews and get you ready to land the job. We won’t rest until we find the perfect match.

The Family Foundation

We are a company that people can anchor to when unplanned events bring about one of life’s personal storms. The Insight Global Family Foundation (IGFF) provides a financial lifeline for those who need help during the most trying periods of their lives. This financial assistance is available to IG’s in-house team and our consultant family. Since launching in January 2020, IGFF has granted more than $620,000 to help 100 people and their families, just when they needed it the most.
