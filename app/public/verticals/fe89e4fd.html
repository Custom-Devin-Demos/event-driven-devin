The Government Technology Agency of Singapore (GovTech Singapore) is the lead agency driving the nation’s Smart Nation initiative (opens in new tab) and public sector digital transformation.

A statutory board under the Ministry of Digital Development and Information (opens in new tab) GovTech Singapore builds tech for public good to improve the lives of Singaporeans, while supporting public agencies in delivering digital services and managing enterprise IT operations. The agency also oversees and safeguards government ICT infrastructure, and regulates public sector ICT procurement, data protection, and cybersecurity.

Our mission

Engineering Digital Government, Making Lives Better.

Learn more about what we do at GovTech.

Our values

Our values guide how we work together and how we deliver on our mission.

They are Ownership, Candour & Openness, and Learning & Growth, anchored by One GovTech.

Ownership

We step forward when we see a problem and take action to solve it.

We take responsibility for what we do, work across teams, and do what it takes to move our shared mission forward.

 

Candour & Openness

We speak honestly, listen with an open mind, and treat one another with respect and empathy.

We create an environment where people can speak up, share information, and challenge ideas constructively. We believe good ideas can come from anyone, anywhere.


Learning & Growth

We stay curious, learn from experience, and help one another grow.

We experiment, learn from both success and failure, and keep building the skills and capabilities we need to serve Singapore better.

 

One GovTech

Our values are grounded in One GovTech.

Across teams, divisions and clusters, we collaborate as one organisation, united by a common purpose and mission.

Our brand identity



Our logo embodies GovTech’s identity as Singapore’s digital transformation leader. Its bold, interconnected lines reflect the way our digital building blocks come together — from individual solutions into a unified network and digital infrastructure. Each product and platform strengthen these connections, symbolising how GovTech powers collaboration, innovation, and progress across government technology. This dynamic and agile visual identity expresses both our purpose and our promise — to engineer digital government and build technology for public good.

Our animated logo illustrates GovTech’s journey as Singapore’s digital transformation leader. Its unfolding motion reveals the building blocks of our digital infrastructure, showing how individual solutions come together as a unified network.

Be part of our mission

Building a Smart Nation and engineering a resilient digital government is a shared journey — one that we undertake together with citizens and the tech industry.

Join our communities

Be part of Singapore’s digital journey with Smart Nation Ambassadors and STACK. As a Smart Nation Ambassador, you’ll test new services, share feedback, provide valuable user feedback to product teams and advocate for the next bound of digital services in Singapore. With STACK, you’ll join a vibrant tech community that learns, builds, and innovates together. By contributing your voice or expertise, you can play a direct role in shaping digital government services that make lives better for all.

Explore public-private partnerships

GovTech partners with the tech industry through open tenders and projects, tapping on the expertise of vendors and technology companies to drive innovation at scale. By collaborating with industry, we strengthen Singapore’s digital government with cutting-edge solutions, accelerate delivery, and build capabilities that improve services for citizens, businesses, and agencies.
