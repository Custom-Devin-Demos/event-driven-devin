Health Plans from VNS Health

For more than two decades, thousands of people have relied on our health plans for outstanding care, freeing them to focus on things besides their health. Which one is right for you?

  • VNS Health EasyCare (HMO)

    A Medicare Advantage plan that offers benefits to make healthy living easier. Extra Help/Low Income Subsidy lowers prescription drug costs for people who qualify.

    Learn About EasyCareFor Existing EasyCare Members

  • VNS Health EasyCare Plus (HMO D-SNP)

    A Medicare Advantage plan for New Yorkers who have both Medicare and Medicaid. It offers more than regular Medicare, including extra benefits that can make it easier to live a healthy lifestyle.

    Learn About EasyCare PlusFor Existing EasyCare Plus Members

  • VNS Health Total (HMO D-SNP)

    A two-in-one plan that combines Medicare Advantage and Medicaid Managed Long Term Care for people who need ongoing help with daily activities to live safely at home. It includes important extra benefits that aren’t covered by regular Medicare.

    Learn About TotalFor Existing Total Members

  • VNS Health MLTC

    A Medicaid Managed Long Term Care plan for people who aren’t eligible for Medicare or who want to keep it separate. It provides long-term services and supports so people who need ongoing help with daily activities can live safely in their homes.

    Learn About MLTCFor Existing MLTC Members

  • SelectHealth

    A specialized Medicaid plan for people living with HIV, individuals who are transgender or gender nonconforming, or homeless individuals regardless of HIV status.

    Learn About SelectHealthFor Existing SelectHealth Members

  • For Health Care Providers

    Digital tools, policies, guidance, and materials to help network providers and others who care for our health plan members.

    Health Plan Resources for Providers

VNS Health Medicare is a Medicare Advantage organization with Medicare and Medicaid contracts, offering HMO D‑SNP and HMO plans. Enrollment in VNS Health Medicare depends on contract renewal.
