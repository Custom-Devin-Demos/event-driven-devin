Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Send

  • What items should I send?
    We accept all brands, though a small number with very low resale value are ineligible for a payout. All items must be clean and in good condition.
  • Which brands don’t earn a payout?
    Some brands are ineligible for a payout. Less than 5% of our 60,000+ brand catalog is ineligible.
  • Should I order a bag or a label?
    It depends on how quickly you want to send. Ready to ship now and have a large box? Order a label. Willing to wait and don’t have a box? Order a bag.
  • How does selling with returns work?
    It’s easy, just opt in to selling when you start a return. You can send up to 30 items when selling with returns.

Fees & Earnings

  • What fees are deducted from my earnings?
    There are no upfront fees. A service fee of $14.99 $0.00 (Standard) or $34.99 (Premium) is deducted from your earnings.
  • How are my items priced?
    We price your items. Our algorithms set prices based on past sales of similar items. You can edit the price, and we reduce prices over time to help your items sell.
  • How can I use my earnings?
    If an item sells, you earn ThredUp shopping credit, or you can cash out. Earnings are issued at different times depending on the item.

After You Send

  • How long does the whole process take?
    It depends on which service you choose. Processing takes 21–28 days for Standard, and most items go live within a day of processing.
  • What happens to items that aren’t listed?
    We accept an average of 60–80% of items. Unlisted items from Standard Clean Outs are reused or responsibly recycled; unlisted items from Premium Clean Outs are returned to you.
  • What happens to items that don’t sell?
    You can reclaim your item for a fee in the final 7 days of its consignment window. Otherwise we give everything else a second life.
