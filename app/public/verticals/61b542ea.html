FAQs

What is the difference between a gift card and an e-gift card?

A gift card is a physical card posted to an address. An e-gift card is sent by e-mail and can be printed or shown on a phone.

When should my Gift card arrive?

E-gift cards are delivered by e-mail. Physical gift cards are sent by post.

Where can I use my Dunelm gift card/e-gift card?

In any Dunelm store and online at dunelm.com.

How long is my gift card/e-gift card valid for?

Please see the terms and conditions supplied with your card.

How much money can I put on a gift card/e-gift card?

You choose the amount when you buy the card.

How do I check the balance on my Dunelm gift card or e-gift card?

Use the balance checker on this page or ask in store.

Can I top-up my gift card/e-gift card after purchase?

Please see the terms and conditions supplied with your card.

Where can I find the gift card/e-gift card Ts and Cs?

Terms and conditions are supplied with your card.

What happens if my gift card is lost, stolen or damaged?

Please contact Customer Services.

What happens if I apply my e-gift Card to an order online, but don't complete the order?

The balance is returned to the card.
