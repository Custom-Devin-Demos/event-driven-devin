Each incident activates a genuine degradation in the app (real slow queries, real timeouts, real memory growth, real intermittent 500s), posts a Datadog-monitor-style alert, and auto-reverts to healthy so the regular demos are unaffected. Beyond Alerts #oncall-bugs SEV-1

Not everything arrives as a machine alert. Customers file tickets with symptoms only, and sometimes everything is on fire at once.

Customer Bug Report
A customer or support agent files an issue through the Acme Support Center portal — no stack trace, just symptoms. Pick a product area and the form pre-fills a real reported symptom; the ticket lands in #oncall-bugs for the Bug Triage Responder to reproduce and root-cause.

Major Incident (SEV-1)
Fires a SEV-1 multi-vertical error spike. With Datadog incident keys configured, this declares a real Datadog incident — Datadog creates the incident channel and the Incident Agent auto-joins. Otherwise the SEV-1 signal posts to the alerts channel.
Application Errors #oncall-alerts

Break a real product. Each card opens the branded page in on-call mode — the customer action (transfer, claim, provision, upgrade) fails with its genuine error UI, and a production alert lands in the alerts channel. The Alert Responder groups duplicates, investigates fresh issues, replies in-thread, and opens a fix PR.
