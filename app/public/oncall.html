Production Alert #oncall-alerts

Opens the real branded product page in on-call mode: the customer action (transfer, claim, provision, upgrade) fails with its genuine error UI and the alert card lands in the alerts channel — no legacy Devin trigger. The Alert Responder groups duplicates, investigates fresh issues, replies in-thread, and opens a fix PR. A “Unique per run” toggle on each page controls duplicate grouping.

Bug Report #oncall-bugs

A customer or support agent files an issue through the Acme Support Center portal — no stack trace, just symptoms — for the Bug Triage Responder to reproduce and root-cause.

Major Incident SEV-1

Fires a SEV-1 multi-vertical error spike. With Datadog incident keys configured, this declares a real Datadog incident (Datadog creates the incident channel and the Incident Agent auto-joins); otherwise it posts the SEV-1 signal to the alerts channel.
